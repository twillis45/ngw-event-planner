## Q1. When do hosts buy planning products, per event type?

General mechanics first, because they govern every row below:

- Etsy listings need roughly **4–8 weeks of ranking history** before they surface on competitive keywords; "the best product in the world will not save a listing that goes live the week of the holiday" ([Listadum Etsy seasonal calendar](https://www.listadum.com/etsy-seasonal-calendar)).
- Experienced template sellers launch **2–3 months before peak demand** ([Templett, "The Best Time to Sell Etsy Templates"](https://templett.com/blog/the-best-time-to-sell-etsy-templates/)); printable sellers plan **6–8 weeks ahead** ([SendOwl](https://www.sendowl.com/blog/tips-and-advice/sell-printables-on-etsy)); seasonal-calendar guidance runs research 90–120 days out and listing 45–60 days out ([SenseCentral](https://sensecentral.com/best-months-to-sell-planners-on-etsy/)).
- Buyers split into **early planners** (search "planner," "template," "checklist," "bundle") and **late buyers** (search "instant download," "printable sign," "last-minute") ([SenseCentral](https://sensecentral.com/best-months-to-sell-planners-on-etsy/)). Our product is an early-planner product; the buying month for us is the planning-START month, which is the correct framing of the question.

**The claim under test — "family reunion planning runs winter-spring for summer events, so a September listing arrives at the end of reunion buying season":**

- **Premise half-confirmed:** the bulk of reunion planning *activity* (invitations, programs, food, activities) does run winter–spring; save-the-dates go out 4–6 months before and invitations 6–8 weeks before a July event, i.e., Jan–May ([Underwood Manor](https://www.underwoodmanor.com/post/the-ultimate-family-reunion-planning-checklist-10-essential-steps-for-2025)).
- **Conclusion refuted:** planning *starts* 9–12 months out — date surveys and venue booking for a July 2027 reunion begin July–October 2026 ([Reunly](https://reunly.io/tools/family-reunion-timeline-generator)) — and an Etsy listing needs 4–8 weeks to rank before demand arrives ([Listadum](https://www.listadum.com/etsy-seasonal-calendar)). A late-September 2026 listing misses the residual 2026 tail but sits perfectly ahead of the January–April 2027 buying bulge. September is the *beginning* of the next cycle, not the end of the last one.
- **Residual uncertainty (UNVERIFIED):** whether the reunion *organizer buys the planning template* at kickoff (fall) or at execution (winter–spring). No direct data on template-purchase timing vs. planning-start timing was found; the safe read is that it does not matter for sequencing, because either way the listing should be live by October to be ranked by January.

## Q2. What is actually launchable in the September–December window?

**Q4 as Etsy's peak — verified and quantified:** Q4 2024 was Etsy's highest-revenue quarter ever at $852M ([Digital Commerce 360](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2025/02/21/etsy-gms-sales-q4-2024/)); November–December alone typically generate **26–32% of annual revenue** for gift-relevant shops ([ListifyAI Etsy holiday calendar](https://www.listifyai.net/blog/etsy-holiday-calendar-2026)); Etsy begins pushing holiday traffic to early-indexed listings from September ([Listadum](https://www.listadum.com/etsy-seasonal-calendar)). Digital products specifically carry the Dec 15–31 last-minute wave and hand off directly into January's planner/organization wave ([eRank Q4 guide](https://help.erank.com/blog/fourth-quarter-holiday-guide-for-handmade-sellers/), [ListifyAI](https://www.listifyai.net/blog/etsy-holiday-calendar-2026)). Caveat: Q4 peak traffic skews to *gifts and decor*; planner-shaped digital products have their own tri-modal peak — Jan (new year), Jul–Sep (back-to-school), Oct–Dec (gift bundles/dated planners) ([InsightAgent planner guide](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/selling-planner-templates-on-etsy)).

**Catchable from a late-September launch:**

1. **Holiday party planner — a front-of-window launch.** Decisions Aug–early Oct, demand peak Oct–Dec. A late-September listing is inside the listing window, slightly behind ideal (early Sept) but viable. (Originally called "the only front-of-window launch"; revised 2026-08-21 — the Thanksgiving hosting planner is a second one, and an October NYE listing a third. See Q6.)
2. **Family reunion planner — early for its window** (see Q1). Launch cost is low; the listing ages into January ranking-ready.
3. **Spring gala planner — early for its window.** Spring gala (Mar–May) committees start 6–9 months out = Sept–Dec ([GrantPipe](https://grantpipe.com/resources/guides/chicago-nonprofit-fundraising-strategy-guide/)).
4. **Evergreen short-lead types (milestone birthday, civilian retirement)** — no window to miss; Q4's general traffic surge plus the review-accumulation clock argue for listing them whenever capacity allows, and Q4 beats Q1.

**NOT launchable / wrong window:**

- **Wedding:** window opens late December–January; a fall listing burns the new-listing boost months early. List late December at the earliest.
- **Graduation:** window opens January–March. The "New Year graduation prep" hypothesis found no support; treat December graduation demand as negligible until measured.
- **Baby shower:** near-evergreen with a spring spike; no Q4 urgency.
- **September cluster niches (tailgate, Oktoberfest, fall festival, military retirement):** their 2026 windows are already closing as of August 21 (Oktoberfest parties are late Sept–early Oct; tailgate season kickoff was August), and listing-ranking lag means a September listing would surface after the season. These are 2027 candidates at best — and Q3 argues most do not earn a template at all.

## Q3. Niche depth check on the September cluster (survey depth)

**Military/federal retirement.**
- *Demand:* Real but unquantified. Editable military retirement invitation templates sell on Etsy in the $7–20 range with Corjl-based editing and multi-branch positioning ([DIY Printables listing](https://www.diyprintable.com/military-retirement-party-invitation-template/), [Modern Pink Paper](https://modernpinkpaper.com/products/military-retirement-party-invitation-personalized)); a cottage content ecosystem exists around ceremony planning (free program/invite templates from a military-spouse photographer, [Bow and Arrow Photography](https://www.bowandarrowphotographystudio.com/military-retirement-ceremony-photography/)). No search-volume figure obtainable without a paid tool.
- *What exists on Etsy:* invitations, decor, and programs — **no ceremony-protocol planner or full-event organizer surfaced** in searches. The listing-count/characterization asked for could not be produced without live Etsy scraping; UNVERIFIED beyond "invitation/decor listings exist in quantity, planners do not surface."
- *Financial-planner pollution:* **Confirmed in practice.** A general-web search on "military retirement" planning terms returns Mercer Advisors, First Command, RBC Wealth Management, AAFMAA, Army Soldier for Life — uniformly pension/TSP/financial content, zero party content ([sample: Mercer Advisors](https://www.merceradvisors.com/solutions/professions/military/), [Soldier for Life](https://soldierforlife.army.mil/Army-Retirement/Retirement-Planning/Change-of-Mission-Newsletter/2025-0401-How-to-Financially-Prepare-for-Retirement)). Any SEO play must be phrased "retirement *ceremony*" or "retirement *party*," never bare "retirement planning." Inside Etsy the pollution is weaker (Etsy buyers search with party intent), but external/Pinterest traffic will suffer.
- *Also:* the fiscal-year-cluster premise is softer than assumed — military financial guidance actively steers members *away* from September retirement dates ([The Military Wallet](https://themilitarywallet.com/retired-military-pay-dates/)).

**Tailgate season planners.** Demand exists and is served: multiple Etsy checklist/planner listings at ~$5, including a 5-page planner set (checklist, RSVP tracker, menu, game notes) ([Etsy tailgate planner listing](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4355638119/tailgate-party-planner-checklist), [Etsy market page](https://www.etsy.com/market/tailgate_list_printable)). The product shape the market rewards is a 1–2 page checklist, not a command-system planner — a poor fit for a premium multi-tab workbook. Low-priority.

**Back-to-school / PTA / booster event tools.** Institutional demand is real but already served free-and-official: National PTA publishes a fundraising template kit ([PTA.org](https://www.pta.org/local-leader-kit/fundraising/templates-tools-resources)), councils publish event-planning worksheets ([Torrance Council of PTAs PDF](https://www.torrancecouncilofptas.org/_files/ugd/332a54_1c09593dda654896a99621f094133507.pdf)), and free guides cover carnivals ([SignUpGenius](https://www.signupgenius.com/resources/carnival-planning-guide), [Carnival Source timeline](https://www.carnivalsource.com/blogs/theme-ideas-using-our-toys-and-novelties/planning-a-school-carnival-a-timeline-that-survives-contact-with-reality)) and booster fundraisers ([BoosterSpark](https://www.boosterspark.com/learn/a/booster-club-fundraiser-event-planning-checklist-12)). Free official competition is the kill signal for a paid template; the salvageable angle is the *gala/auction fundraiser* run by a small parent committee, which folds into the fundraiser/gala product rather than standing alone.

**Oktoberfest party planning.** Etsy carries a healthy invitation-template market (multiple editable Canva/Corjl listings, [Etsy Oktoberfest invites market](https://www.etsy.com/market/oktoberfest_invites)) but demand is invitation/decor-shaped, single-evening, and tightly seasonal (late Sept–early Oct). No planner-shaped demand surfaced. Skip as a template; weak as a playbook.

**Fall festival / harvest party.** Served by free institutional checklists ([SignUpGenius festival checklist](https://www.signupgenius.com/resources/festival-checklist)); demand overlaps school-carnival (free competition, above) and home Halloween/fall decor (decor-shaped, peaks late Aug–Oct per [Listadum](https://www.listadum.com/etsy-seasonal-calendar)). Marginal.

## Q5. Sanity-check the July rankings against seasonality

July ranking: family reunion > retirement party > milestone birthday > wedding-last.

- **Reunion-first: HOLDS, reframed.** Not because September is inside the reunion buying season (it is not, for volume) but because (a) the fall is the planning-kickoff edge of the 2027 cycle, and (b) the 12–18-month review-velocity lag from the July audit means the product that most needs a head start should list first. Reunion remains the flagship; its harvest is January–April 2027.
- **Retirement second: HOLDS for the civilian evergreen product; DEMOTE the military September angle.** The fiscal-year cluster is unquantified, the 2026 window is already too close to catch (event ≤6 weeks out means buyers are past the planning-product stage by the time a late-Sept listing ranks), and search pollution is confirmed. Civilian retirement is evergreen — list it in Q4 for review accumulation.
- **Milestone birthday third: HOLDS.** Evergreen; list in Q4 opportunistically.
- **Wedding last: HOLDS as a fall decision, but wedding gets a hard January date, not an indefinite deferral.** Wedding demand is the largest absolute market of the set ([iimagined category table](https://iimagined.ai/blog/sell-digital-products-on-etsy-complete-success-guide): wedding/party printables highest revenue potential) and has the sharpest, most predictable window (Dec engagements → Jan planning → Mar–Jun stationery peak). "Last" should mean "listed late December/January," which is only ~3 months after launch.
- **NEW ENTRANT the July audit could not see: holiday party.** It was ranked nowhere, but it is the only niche whose buying window a late-September launch actually opens into, and Q4 carries 26–32% of annual revenue. It should jump the queue to first-listed (not necessarily flagship).

**Resulting sequence (as amended 2026-08-21):** Late Sept: holiday party + Thanksgiving hosting (with Friendsgiving variant) + family reunion → Oct: gala/fundraiser (small-committee) + NYE planner (+ optional Halloween late-buyer hedge) → Nov: milestone birthday + retirement (civilian) → late Dec/Jan: wedding + graduation → Feb–Mar: baby shower refresh, reunion promotion → Jun–Jul 2027: Halloween planner proper. (Full table in the executive answer; October–December evidence in Q6.)

## Q6. October–December hosting events (added 2026-08-21 after review)

The original study jumped from the September cluster to a generic "holiday party" and skipped the fall-winter hosting calendar. Same discipline as Q1–Q3: event window vs planning-START (buying) window, planner-shaped vs decor-shaped demand, Etsy/free competition depth, and 2026 catchability from a late-September or October listing.

### Q6.1 Halloween parties

- **Scale — the largest US fall host-side event by spending.** NRF's annual survey (Prosper Insights, 8,045 consumers, Sept 2–9, 2025) put 2025 Halloween spending at a **record $13.1 billion**, up from $11.6B in 2024, at a record $114 per person ([NRF press release](https://nrf.com/media-center/press-releases/nrf-consumer-survey-finds-halloween-spending-to-reach-record-13-1-billion)). NRF releases each year's figures in late September ([NRF Halloween data hub](https://nrf.com/research-insights/holiday-data-and-trends/halloween)) — the 2026 number was not yet published at this writing. On the hosting side specifically, 29% of hosts host a Halloween gathering — behind Thanksgiving (66%) and Christmas/Hanukkah (59%) but ahead of most non-holiday occasions ([HomePage News 2026](https://www.homepagenews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/26_HPN_At-Home-Entertaining.pdf)).
- **Planning/buying window — earlier than the event by a lot.** 49% of consumers began Halloween shopping in September or earlier in 2025, up from 47% ([NRF](https://nrf.com/media-center/press-releases/nrf-consumer-survey-finds-halloween-spending-to-reach-record-13-1-billion)). Retailer order data: 60% of Halloween orders are placed before October, with the season peaking the week of September 30 ([Factory Direct Party](https://factorydirectparty.com/blogs/party-ideas/halloween-party-ideas)). Host-side guides put party planning start at 6–8 weeks out with invitations 3–4 weeks out because late-October weekends fill fast ([Simple Everyday Lists](https://simpleeverydaylists.com/halloween-party-checklist/), [Factory Direct Party](https://factorydirectparty.com/blogs/party-ideas/halloween-party-ideas)). So the planner-buying window is roughly **late August–early October**, confirming the "planning starts September" premise.
- **Etsy landscape — decor/SVG mass, thin planner layer.** 2026 Halloween demand on Etsy concentrates in five product types — shirts, SVGs, stickers, decor, party supplies ([ShopFoundry Halloween trends](https://www.shopfoundry.app/trends/etsy-halloween)); seller education treats Halloween digital products as invitations, games, activity bundles, and decor first ([Snazzy Designs](https://snazzydesignsforever.com/seasonal-digital-products-to-sell-on-etsy/), [SenseCentral Halloween guide](https://sensecentral.com/how-to-sell-halloween-party-printables/)). Planner/checklist demand exists but is heavily served **free** (Eventbrite's organizer checklist, Maple Planners' free printable, many blog checklists — [Eventbrite](https://www.eventbrite.com/blog/halloween-party-checklist/), [Maple Planners](https://www.mapleplanners.com/download/halloween-party-checklist)). A paid Halloween product needs to be a full party-planning bundle (theme + guest + menu + day-of), not a bare checklist. No live Etsy listing census was run; depth characterization beyond this is UNVERIFIED.
- **2026 catchability — NO for the early-planner product; the window is already lost.** Seller-side data is unusually unanimous: Halloween listings must be live by **early August** (some say end of July) because September is the peak search month and October listings compete against shops with 6–8 weeks of traction ([ShopFoundry](https://www.shopfoundry.app/trends/etsy-halloween), [markty.ai](https://www.markty.ai/en/academy/blog/the-complete-halloween-content-calendar-for-etsy-sellers), [Louplr](https://www.louplr.com/blog/etsy-seasonal-selling-calendar)). A late-September 2026 listing lands *after* peak search, with zero ranking history, against two-month-old competitors. The only honest 2026 angle is the **late-buyer wave**: in the first half of October urgency shoppers pivot to instant-download digital files ([markty.ai](https://www.markty.ai/en/academy/blog/the-complete-halloween-content-calendar-for-etsy-sellers)), and a listing keyworded "instant download / last minute Halloween party" could pick up scraps while banking history — Etsy sellers are advised to keep seasonal listings live year over year precisely because ranking carries forward ([Snazzy Designs](https://snazzydesignsforever.com/seasonal-digital-products-to-sell-on-etsy/)). **Verdict: bank for 2027 (design June, list by early August 2027); the optional October 2026 hedge is a ranking deposit, not a revenue play.**

### Q6.2 Friendsgiving

- **Demand — real, young-skewing, and distinctly host-shaped.** No official statistics exist; recent surveys range widely — up to ~20% of Americans per NPR's reading of online surveys ([NPR](https://www.npr.org/2025/11/19/nx-s1-5605483/friendsgiving-thanksgiving-friends-history-tips)), nearly half planning to *attend* one per a 2023 brand survey ([Green Giant/PRNewswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/green-giant-survey-finds-nearly-1-in-2-americans-will-celebrate-friendsgiving-this-year-301989365.html)), and 46% of 13–39-year-olds planning to host a holiday gathering ([YPulse](https://www.ypulse.com/article/2024/10/16/gen-z-is-even-more-into-friendsgiving-than-millennials/)). One contrarian 2024 poll showed intent collapsing year-over-year ([Talker Research](https://talkerresearch.com/are-americans-over-holiday-gatherings/)) — treat magnitude as UNVERIFIED, direction (large, sustained since ~2015) as solid. The structure is exactly the app's shape: an organizer, a potluck matrix, dish assignment, dietary tracking — most Friendsgivings are potluck-style with hosts assigning dishes ([Axios/Evite](https://www.axios.com/2019/11/28/friendsgiving-increase-popularity-changes-society), avg party size 21).
- **Window.** Events cluster in the two weeks around Thanksgiving; sign-up sheets go out 2–3 weeks before ([Whocan](https://www.whocan.org/en/friendsgiving-potluck-sign-up-sheet/)) → buying window **early–mid November**, confirming the premise. A late-September listing is comfortably early.
- **Competition — the potluck sheet itself is free everywhere.** The Kitchn, LoveToKnow, 101 Planners, Whocan, and bloggers all give away Friendsgiving sign-up sheets and hosting kits ([The Kitchn](https://www.thekitchn.com/this-is-the-ultimate-sign-up-sheet-for-potlucks-251072), [101 Planners](https://www.101planners.com/potluck-sign-up-sheet/), [Alex Alexander](https://alexalexander.com/friendsgiving-potluck-sign-up-sheet/)). Per the Q3 doctrine, free competition kills a standalone paid sheet. **Verdict: no standalone template; ship as a Friendsgiving/potluck VARIANT inside the Thanksgiving hosting planner**, where the paid value is the integrated system (menu math + potluck matrix + timeline), which the free single-sheet ecosystem does not offer. For the APP, the fit is direct: the shared-dishes/food-plan intelligence already models dish assignment.

### Q6.3 Thanksgiving hosting

- **Demand — the most-hosted occasion in America.** Thanksgiving leads all hosted occasions at **66%** of hosts, ahead of Christmas/Hanukkah at 59%; 58% of consumers hosted a gathering in the past year, and 26% would use websites/digital tools to prepare ([HomePage News 2026](https://www.homepagenews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/26_HPN_At-Home-Entertaining.pdf)).
- **The pain is minute-level day-of timing — the run-of-show product.** Among US holiday cooks, the single biggest headache is "timing the meal and all of their dishes so it's all done and ready to be served at the same time" (**56%**), with oven-space conflicts and multi-dish simultaneity the top named stressors ([Ipsos/Samsung](https://www.ipsos.com/en-us/despite-planning-ahead-holiday-meals-many-hosts-worry-about-getting-all-their-dishes-table-time) — 2011, old but structural). The dominant content genre is the backward-scheduled timeline: menu → shopping lists → make-ahead calendar → hour-by-hour day-of schedule ([America's Test Kitchen](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/articles/7783-thanksgiving-make-ahead-timeline), [My Kitchen Escapades oven-timing planner](https://www.mykitchenescapades.com/thanksgiving-cooking-schedule/), [I Heart Naptime planner PDF](https://www.iheartnaptime.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/I-Heart-Naptime-Thanksgiving-Planner-2024.pdf)). A dedicated app ([Time 2 Table](https://time2table.com/)) and a $7/month AI hosting assistant ([Lemon Price](https://www.lemonprice.co/hosting-assistant/)) now sell exactly this — backward-computed minute-level schedules with oven-conflict detection — which is both demand proof and a note that this is one of the few event types where **day-of run-of-show IS the core product**, mapping one-to-one onto the app's run-of-show engine.
- **Window.** Planning starts 2–3 weeks before Thanksgiving (menu, turkey order — [ATK](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/articles/7783-thanksgiving-make-ahead-timeline)), so the buying window is **late October–mid November**; seller guidance says list Thanksgiving products by September ([Louplr](https://www.louplr.com/blog/etsy-seasonal-selling-calendar)).
- **Competition.** Free timelines are abundant (ATK, food blogs, free printables); Etsy carries Thanksgiving planners inside broader holiday-planner bundles. The free material is static PDF — the paid/premium angle is the *computed* schedule (guest count → quantities, serve time → backward timeline, one oven → conflict resolution), which free printables structurally cannot do. No listing census; Etsy depth beyond "planners exist, mostly bundled" is UNVERIFIED.
- **2026 catchability — YES.** A late-September listing sits ~8 weeks ahead of the buying window with time to rank. This is the second front-of-window launch the original study missed. **Verdict: list late September 2026, bundled with the Friendsgiving potluck variant.**

### Q6.4 New Year's Eve parties

- **Demand.** NYE celebration intent rose 2 points into 2025 — one of the two biggest gains among 14 tracked holidays — with 51% of celebrators buying alcohol and 15% ordering food ([Numerator via Chain Store Age](https://chainstoreage.com/numerator-heres-how-consumers-plan-celebrate-key-holidays-2025)). Host-side planning content is planner-shaped (guest list/RSVP, budget, menu, drink math, countdown-hour programming) rather than decor-shaped ([RSVPify](https://rsvpify.com/new-years-eve-party-guide/), [greatEvent checklist](https://www.greatevent.com/new-years-checklist-plan-memorable-party/)). No hosting-rate figure found; magnitude vs Halloween/Thanksgiving UNVERIFIED but clearly smaller.
- **Window.** Guidance converges on starting 4–6 weeks before December 31 (8–10 for 20+ guests), invitations 3–4 weeks out ([PlanWiz](https://blog.planwiz.app/new-year-eve-celebration-party-planner/), [greatEvent](https://www.greatevent.com/new-years-checklist-plan-memorable-party/)) → buying window **late November–mid December**, confirming the premise.
- **Competition.** Multi-page NYE party planner printables sell on Etsy (guest list, budgets, menu, potluck plan, to-do — e.g., [White Spruce Studio](https://whitesprucestudio.com/products/printable-new-year-party-planner)); free checklists exist but less densely than for Thanksgiving. Depth UNVERIFIED without a census.
- **2026 catchability — YES.** An October listing gives 6–8 weeks of ranking before the late-November buying window and rides peak Q4 traffic. **Verdict: list October 2026.** Note the distinction from the holiday party planner: same quarter, different event mechanics (countdown-hour programming, midnight anchor); ship as a distinct listing or a clearly named variant, not a re-skin, per the no-duplicate-surfaces rule.

### Q6.5 Dia de los Muertos (demand check only — flagged)

### Q6.6 Christmas dinner hosting and Hanukkah (distinct from "holiday party")

Christmas *dinner* hosting is a different event from the cocktail/office "holiday party" the existing product targets: 36% of US respondents cook Christmas dinner themselves and 34% cook it as a family ([Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/1548524/christmas-dinner-preparations-in-the-united-states/)); Christmas/Hanukkah gatherings are hosted by 59% of hosts ([HomePage News 2026](https://www.homepagenews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/26_HPN_At-Home-Entertaining.pdf)). ## Confidence and gaps

**Well-evidenced:** Q4 revenue concentration and the Etsy 4–8-week ranking lag; the 2–3-months-ahead listing doctrine (multiple independent seller-education sources); engagement-season statistics and the January wedding-planning spike; corporate holiday party decision timing (Aug–early Oct, multiple independent event-industry sources); gala planning lead times (6–12 months, three independent sources); family reunion 9–12-month kickoff (multiple sources); financial-planner pollution of military retirement search (directly reproduced).

**Thin:** monthly distribution of retirement parties and milestone birthdays (asserted evergreen from absence of evidence — a known weak inference); the magnitude of the military fiscal-year ceremony cluster (one source even argues against Sept retirements); whether reunion organizers buy templates at kickoff (fall) or execution (winter); tailgate and Oktoberfest demand quantification; everything about Rosh Hashanah dinner and Grandparents Day demand (essentially unresearched this pass); the December-graduation Q4 window (no support found, but absence was not exhaustively proven).

**Added by the 2026-08-21 amendment — well-evidenced:** NRF Halloween spending scale and early-shopping share (primary source, 8,045-consumer survey); the early-August Halloween listing deadline (three independent seller sources plus retailer order data); Thanksgiving as the most-hosted occasion and the 56% dish-timing pain point; NYE and Thanksgiving planning-start windows (multiple independent sources each). **Thin:** Friendsgiving participation magnitude (surveys range ~17% to ~50%, one shows collapse); NYE hosting rate; Etsy listing-depth for all four (no census run — same gap as Q3); Dia de los Muertos and Hanukkah planner demand (essentially unresearched, and both flagged regardless); the 2026 NRF Halloween figure (not yet published — recheck late September 2026).

- eRank/Marmalead: actual Etsy monthly search volumes and 12-month seasonality curves for "family reunion planner," "holiday party planner," "military retirement party," "tailgate planner" — this would convert every "UNVERIFIED magnitude" above into a number.

- Live Etsy scrape or manual count: top-20 listing census for "military retirement party" and "family reunion planner" (count, price, review count, planner-vs-decor split) — the Q3 characterization is qualitative without it.
- Human test: a $0 Pinterest/SEO probe on "reunion planning kickoff" content in September–October 2026 would directly test whether template buying happens at kickoff or execution.

**Revised 2026-08-21.** The app's 41 playbooks include Holiday Party, The Cookout, Watch Party, and Kwanzaa Gathering — but no Halloween, no Friendsgiving/Thanksgiving-hosting, and no New Year's Eve type. It drops below the two new entrants only on raw host-population scale, not on fit.

Smaller than the three above; do not let it duplicate the Holiday Party surface — it earns a type only if its run-of-show meaningfully differs, which the midnight-anchored evening suggests it does.
