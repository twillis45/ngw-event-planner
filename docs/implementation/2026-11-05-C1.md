Date: November 5, 2026. Production base: `d22a9683`. Worktree: `wt-paid-web-rc`, branch `codex/paid-web-rc-2026-11-03`. No deployment or billing/profile change.

## Evidence and root cause

Audit/planning evidence is `origin/main-2utz0n` through `ac1d085d`. Its divergence from production is documentation only; no runtime code was merged from it.

`hostSpending` added manual actuals, NGW estimates, and vendor balances without resolving their overlap. Paying a vendor subtracted the balance from commitments without recording the payment as spent. The Margaret retirement sample reproduced the audit exactly: $16,627 spoken for against $13,600; marking Hearthstone paid reduced that number by $3,150 while spent did not move.

Desired, expected, committed, and spent now have distinct meanings:

- Desired: the canonical budget target (`budgetFor`).
- Expected: contract, otherwise host allocation, otherwise NGW estimate, resolved once per category.
- Committed: obligations and purchases, including amounts already paid. An allocation alone is not a contract.
- Spent: payment/purchase records, counted once per category. Conflicting budget/vendor records remain visible; they are not rewritten automatically. Estimated checked-off purchases retain their estimate disclosure.

This refines the plan's wording: host allocations contribute to expected, not automatically to committed. Explicit budgetCategory links override category aliases. Unknown labels remain distinct. Real purchases survive an allocation; unpriced bookings cannot replace known money with zero; paid vendors survive shopping-status changes.

## Runtime changes

The existing money sheet and After summary consume the owned category breakdown. Forecasts are labeled separately from spoken-for money. Recovery suggestions use the expected forecast and say so. Payment reconciliation compares recorded vendor payments with linked actuals, never an allocation treated as debt.

Log a payment uses patchEvent and the existing budget array. Hosts can record their first expense, add to a manual entry, reject invalid amounts, reload, and Undo. Contracted payments route to their vendor records. The form checks the upcoming C6 `{ok:false}` writer contract and keeps its values on refusal; C6 integration is required before failed-save payment truth is closed.
