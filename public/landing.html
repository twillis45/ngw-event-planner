What it does — plainly

It carries the plan, so you don't have to.

No dashboards to study. You open it, it tells you the one thing worth doing today, and everything it says is checked against what's really true.

01

One thing at a time

Not a wall of red. Not fourteen reminders. The top of your screen says the single next thing — book the tables, confirm the count, buy the drinks — and takes you straight to it. And when it says you're in good shape, it's because you actually are.

02

It remembers who's bringing what

Aunt Lisa said she's got dessert? Great — but the plan keeps a spot for it until you've confirmed with her, and doesn't call it done until the tray is actually on the table. Nobody gets embarrassed, nothing gets forgotten.

03

The day before, it gets quiet

All the planning noise folds down into one page: what still matters, who to check in with, what to stop worrying about. And on the day itself it stops talking about planning entirely — it just walks the day with you.

04

It does the awkward writing for you

The invite text, the "still on for Saturday?" note to the DJ, the final details message with the address and what to bring — drafted from your real plan, in plain words, for you to look over and send.

Our word — three promises

Built for your family's business to stay your family's business.

We won't make things up

If it doesn't know, it says so.

No invented prices, no fake suggestions, no pretend smartness. Every number on your screen comes from something you told it or something real.

We won't nag you

Calm, always.

One nudge at a time, and only when it matters. Finished things fold themselves away. If you don't want to track RSVPs, it will never bring them up.

We won't share your people

Your folks stay yours.

Who's bringing what stays between you and the app. Vendors see only what they need for the day. Guests see only what you choose to send.
